Using Contour Study

Line drawing: The left viewport traces the visible outer contour and the boundary where surfaces turn away from the main light. It updates with the 3D view, model orientation, lens, and light direction. Light-side planes records noticeable plane changes across a broader portion of the light. Surface noise is filtered and small line gaps are joined into solid black strokes. Increase Plane detail to include smaller changes or planes farther from shadow; decrease it for fewer lines. Uncheck Light-side planes for just contours and form-shadow boundaries. Cast shadows and texture details are not included. Form shadows receive a gray tone matching the reference model’s shadow value; lines stay black on white.

View: Drag with one finger to rotate. Pinch to zoom, or move two fingers to pan. With a mouse, drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, and right-drag to pan.

Models: Upload ZIP, GLB, glTF, OBJ, or STL files. ZIPs are unpacked automatically, including ZIPs inside ZIPs. If several models are found, choose one from the dropdown. For loose glTF files, select the model and its .bin / texture files together. OBJ and STL use a neutral material.

Camera: Random Orientation rotates the model into a new angle. Reset View centers it in the frame. Lens values use a full-frame camera with a square crop. Switching lenses preserves your viewing angle and adjusts camera distance to keep the model framed: wide lenses exaggerate depth and long lenses give a flatter view.

Lighting: Roll the sphere with your thumb to move the light around the model. Keep swiping to reach the back or underside. Arrow keys also move the light when the sphere is focused. Adjust brightness, light color, and background color below it. Reset restores the light; Randomize changes its direction, color, and brightness.

Files stay on your device and are not saved after reload. Use standard unencrypted ZIPs under 100 MB. Compressed glTF geometry and KTX2 textures are not supported yet.
